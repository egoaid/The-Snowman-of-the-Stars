About The Snowman of the Stars

A calm 3D sandbox you can play in your browser, set in a natural world where a snowman lives. There are three ways to play: Walk Mode (explore on foot), Watch Mode (watch the snowman go about its day) and Edit Mode (move logs and other things around). Day and night, the seasons and the weather change, and every ambient sound is synthesized live. Create users and worlds and save each world in this device’s browser. Free, no installation needed, and works on smartphones.

⚙️ Settings

🔊 Sound

Ambient sounds are the river, rain, wind, insects, birds, campfire and thunder. Sound effects are footsteps, picking up and placing things, the chest, logs and the star chimes.

🌐 Language / 言語

💧 Water

If your device runs slowly, choose “Standard” or “Off”.
The night sky and the moon are reflected in the river. Not shown when water quality is “Off”.

📱 Screen

🖥️ Display

🌦️ Season & weather

In “Auto”, the weather follows the season: more rain in the rainy season, more thunderstorms in typhoon season, and snow in winter.

🌳 Tree density

Changes how many branches and leaves the big tree beside the tent has. Changing it reloads the world to rebuild the tree (your world is saved).

🕐 Time

🕐 --:--
One in-game day passes every 20 minutes of real time (about the same pace as Minecraft).

Controls

Computer
WASD: move / Mouse: look / Space: jump / Shift: run (while held)
V: switch view (first / third person) / E: open or close the inventory / 1–9, wheel: select hotbar slot
Left click: act on what you’re aiming at / Esc: stop walking

Smartphone
Left joystick: move / Drag the screen: look / A: jump
👝: open or close the inventory / ✦: act on what you’re aiming at / 📷: switch view / ✕ at the top right: stop walking
Tap a hotbar icon: choose what to hold
